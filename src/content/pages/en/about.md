## Why interpretability

I care about interpretability first as an engineering problem. If a system makes decisions, I want to be able to look inside and see why. A model we can only evaluate from the outside is a model we trust out of habit.

It is also an ethical concern. The more autonomy we give these systems, the more it matters that someone can check what they are actually doing, not just what they say they are doing. I build agents, so this is not an abstract point for me.

And it is a philosophical interest. Philosophy of mind spent a long time asking what it means to represent, to remember, to know. Language models don't answer those questions, but they turn some of them into experiments. I find AI most interesting as a mirror: working out how it works is also a way of asking how we do.

I don't have grand predictions. I would rather measure things.
